MindMate is an AI-powered study coach for students.

Core principle:
The app should proactively tell students what to do next instead of waiting for them to ask.

MVP Screens:
1. Welcome
2. Dashboard
3. AI Coach
4. Study Planner
5. Profile

Dashboard:
- Your Next Step (largest card)
- AI Recommendation
- Upcoming Exams
- Daily Progress
- Study Streak

AI Coach:
- Today's Coaching
- Chat interface
- Suggested actions

Study Planner:
- Today's tasks
- Weekly planner
- Add task
- Complete tasks

Design:
- Mobile-first
- Modern
- Blue and white theme
- Clean and minimal

Priority:
Working functionality over perfect UI.
